About this site

Why might I see more than one federal member?

Postcodes weren’t designed around electoral boundaries, so one postcode can cover parts of several electorates. We show every member whose electorate overlaps your postcode. To confirm which electorate you live in, use the Australian Electoral Commission’s Find my electorate tool.

Where does the information come from?

Names, parties, electorates, roles and photos come from OpenAustralia, a project that publishes parliamentary information. It can lag behind recent changes such as by-elections or ministry reshuffles.

Who runs this site?

It’s an independent project. It isn’t run by, or connected to, any government body, political party or candidate, and it doesn’t save or share the postcodes you enter.
